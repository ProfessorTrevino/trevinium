Everything it does

Twelve detectors. One app.

Your phone is already packed with sensors. Trevinium Vision turns them into instruments and shows all of them at once.

01ULTRA

Ultrasound viewer

See sound above human hearing, from 17 kHz up, as a live scrolling spectrum.

02ULTRA

Beacon & tone detector

03BT

Bluetooth scanner

Every broadcasting device around you on a live radar, with an estimated distance.

04BT

05WIFI

Wi-Fi analyzer

Every network around you and how strong its signal is.

06WIFI

07MAG

Why it's different

Why download twelve apps?

The app stores are full of single-purpose detectors: a Bluetooth scanner, a hidden-camera finder, an EMF meter, a Wi-Fi analyzer, a satellite viewer. Each one does one thing, in its own screen.

Trevinium Vision runs all of them together in one camera view. The edges of the screen glow in the color of whatever is most active right now, so you see the whole invisible picture at a glance.
