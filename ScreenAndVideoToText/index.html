What it does

Two buttons. Every word.

A small two-button strip floats at the edge of your screen, over any app. Drag it anywhere and it tucks itself against the side.

BUTTON 01 · THE SOUND WAVE

Hear the video.
Get the words.

Tap it while a video plays. It listens to the sound your phone is playing, not the room, and turns the speech into clean text.

  • Real sentences, with punctuation and capitals
  • Transcribes as it listens, so the text is ready the moment you stop
  • Up to 10 minutes per clip; at the limit it stops and transcribes by itself
  • Lands on your clipboard and saves to History
BUTTON 02 · THE T

See the text.
Copy it.

Tap it and every word visible on your screen is read and copied, from any app, any picture, any paused video.

  • Captions burned into video, text in photos, screenshots, memes
  • Apps that block long-press and copy
  • One tap: no cropping, no switching apps
  • Leaves out the phone's own buttons and bars, so the text comes out clean

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. The app has no internet permission at all. Speech recognition and text reading run on your phone, so it works in airplane mode.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase. Buy it once and it's yours.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 10 and newer. Capturing the sound that other apps play needs Android 10 or later.

What languages does it understand?

Speech-to-text understands English. The T button reads the words shown on your screen.

How long can a clip be?

Up to 10 minutes per clip. At the limit it stops and transcribes automatically. For longer videos, start another clip.

Why does Android ask permission every time?

Android asks before any app can see your screen, at the start of every session. That's Android protecting your privacy, and no app can skip it.

What if an app blocks its sound?

A few apps don't allow their audio to be captured. Turn on Microphone fallback and it listens through the mic for those.

How do I turn it off?

Drag the buttons onto the X, tap Stop in History, or use the notification. Your saved captures stay.
