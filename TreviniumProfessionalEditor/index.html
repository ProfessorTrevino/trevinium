See it in action

Real screens. Real tools.

Every screen below is from the Android preview build, running on a phone.

COLOR

Grade on a live picture of your shot.

Drag the color wheel and your frame changes right in front of you, with a professional scope underneath. No numbers to type.

  • Color-balance wheel, plus exposure, contrast, shadows, midtones, highlights, saturation, temperature and vignette
  • RGB parade, histogram and luma waveform: tap to switch
  • Grade one clip or the whole film. Import .cube LUTs and save your own looks
AUDIO

Left and Right, the way pros mix.

Every clip's sound is split into Audio L and Audio R lanes automatically, each with its own waveform and its own level.

  • Set each side on its own, or turn a side off completely
  • Import music, voice and sound effects, or record a 48 kHz voice-over at the playhead
  • Ducking, a master mixer with EQ and limiter, and one-knob dialogue cleanup
WORDS

Captions and cuts, straight from the words.

Speech recognition runs on your phone, not a server. Turn speech into captions, or edit the clip by striking words from its transcript.

  • Auto captions made entirely on the device (English)
  • Edit by transcript: strike words to cut them, with false starts suggested
  • Caption styles, and SRT export and import
TITLES

Titles and rolling credits, previewed on your frame.

Type the words, pick a style and see it on your own shot before you add it. Set exactly when it comes on and goes off.

  • Gold, white and yellow styles, plus rolling end credits
  • Height on screen and size sliders
  • An on-screen range bar sets In and Out
TRANSITIONS

See the transition before you choose it.

Each style plays on your own two clips, looping, so you pick by eye, not by name.

  • Cut, dip to black and cross-dissolve
  • Lengths of 0.25, 0.5 and 1 second
  • One tap applies it to every cut in the film
SPEED

Slow motion, fast motion and smooth ramps.

Pick a speed from a quarter speed up to triple, or ramp from one speed to another with an easing curve.

  • 0.25x to 3x constant speed
  • Speed ramps with classic, ease-in, ease-out and ease-in-out curves
  • Reverse a clip or freeze a frame
FRAMING

Fit any screen, from cinema to vertical.

Letterbox or fill, crop the edges, rotate and flip, with a live preview of the result.

  • 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 1.85:1 and 2.39:1 canvases
  • Auto-reframe that finds the face and keeps it centered
  • Safe zones for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
EXPORT

Up to 4K, saved straight to your gallery.

Choose the size and frame rate, then H.264 or HEVC. No watermark, ever, and nothing is uploaded.

  • 480p, 720p, 1080p and 2160p (4K)
  • Source frame rate, or 23.976, 24, 25, 30, 50 and 60 fps
  • Send the timeline to Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve
Everything it does

Every tool. Every feature.

Built the way professional editors work, sized for one hand. Every setting is a wheel, a slider or a picture of your own shot. You never type a number.

01EDIT

Timeline & editing

A magnetic timeline that feels like a desktop editor.

  • Magnetic timeline with snapping, pinch to zoom and a gold playhead
  • Trim by grabbing the edge. No handles in the way of precise cuts, and the trim previews live
  • Split at the playhead, delete and ripple edits that keep everything after in sync
  • Long-press to lift any clip, title or sound and drag it in time
  • Source monitor: play the original clip, mark In and Out, then insert it at the playhead
  • Speed: constant speed, or ramps with curves (classic, ease in, ease out, ease in-out)
  • Reverse a clip and freeze a frame
  • Jog wheel and J / K / L shuttle with frame-by-frame steps, plus keyboard shortcuts
  • Copy, paste and duplicate clips, with full undo and redo
  • Full-screen adjustment panels with a live picture of what you're changing pinned on top, and Apply always in reach
  • No number typing: wheels, sliders and range bars over your own frames and waveforms. Double-tap any control to reset it
  • Portrait or landscape: in landscape the monitor and timeline sit side by side with the tools
02AUDIO

Sound

Audio laid out the way pros mix it.

  • Left and Right audio lanes, split automatically, each with its own waveform. Mono sounds are marked Mono
  • Set each side on its own: Left and Right levels for every clip
  • Music, voice and sound-effect tracks with volume, pan, fade in and fade out
  • Record a voice-over in 48 kHz right at the playhead
  • Automatic ducking: music dips under voice
  • Master mixer: 3-band EQ, compression, peak limiter, and loudness normalizing to −14 LUFS
  • Clean up dialogue with one knob: noise reduction learned from your clip, de-esser, mud cut, presence and leveling, with Before and After
  • Sync production audio, and unlink, extract or mute a clip's sound
  • Render a preview mix to hear the finished sound before you export
03COLOR

Color grading

Grade on a live picture of your own shot.

  • Color-balance wheel and sliders for exposure, contrast, shadows, midtones, highlights, saturation, temperature and vignette
  • Scopes: RGB parade, histogram and luma waveform
  • Looks previewed on your frame, plus saved looks you can reuse
  • Import .cube LUTs and shape RGB curves
  • Per-clip grades or the whole film, with copy, paste and reset
  • Adjustment layers with their own grade, film grain and camera shake
  • Chroma key for green screen and blue screen
04WORDS

Captions, titles & transcript

Speech recognition that runs on your phone, not on a server.

  • Auto captions made on the device (English)
  • Edit by transcript: strike words in the text and the cuts are made for you. Likely false starts and stutters are flagged
  • Remove silence with live sliders that show every cut and the time saved before you apply
  • Titles in gold, white or yellow, plus rolling credits
  • Caption styles, and SRT import and export
05MOTION

Layers, motion & framing

Composite, animate and reframe for any screen.

  • Image overlays you drag and pinch into place, with size, opacity and rotation
  • Keyframe overlays with a curve editor for smooth easing
  • Picture-in-picture video
  • Transitions: cut, dip to black and cross-dissolve, previewed on your own clips
  • Crop, fit, rotate and flip
  • Every shape of screen: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 1.85:1 and 2.39:1
  • Auto-reframe that finds the face and keeps it centered
  • Safe zones for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus rule-of-thirds framing guides
  • Full-screen playback with a double-tap on the picture
06EXPORT

Export & workflow

Finish on the phone, or keep going on the desktop.

  • Export up to 4K at 23.976, 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps, in H.264 or HEVC, straight to your gallery
  • No watermark, ever
  • Send your timeline to the desktop: Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and EDL
  • Edit from an external SSD or card: projects live in a folder that opens on another device without relinking
  • Collect media, relink missing files and project archives
  • Named projects and automatic drafts, so you pick up right where you left off
